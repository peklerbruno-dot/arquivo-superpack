SICHA
COM BASE NO TRABALHO DO GRINHO

John Locke's empiricism is the theory that all knowledge originates from sensory experience and reflection, rather than innate ideas. He proposed the concept of the tabula rasa (blank slate), asserting that the mind is empty at birth and filled through observation of the external world (sensation) and internal mental processes (reflection). Locke distinguished between primary qualities (inherent properties of objects like shape and motion) and secondary qualities (subjective perceptions like color and sound), which arise from these primary qualities.
